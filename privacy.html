GameSense Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

GameSense is an iPhone and Apple Watch app for tracking your soccer matches. This policy explains what data the app uses, where that data is kept, and what we will and won't do with it.

In short

  • Today, your match and health data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We don't run servers that receive it.
  • We will never sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.
  • If we add accounts or cloud features later, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before any health data leaves your device.

Data the app uses

Health data (Apple Health / HealthKit). If you allow it, GameSense reads the following from Apple Health:

  • heart rate and resting heart rate
  • workouts
  • walking and running distance
  • active energy (calories)
  • workout routes, which include location data recorded during a workout

The app uses this data to show your matches, work out match intensity and recovery, and track your progress. GameSense can also save your match workouts to Apple Health. You choose which data types to allow, and you can change that at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings.

Match data you create. This includes match names, scores, start and end times, your positions on the pitch, and time spent on the bench.

Purchase data. If you subscribe to GameSense Unlimited, Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details. See “Service providers” below.

Notifications. The app can send local notifications, for example when a match's intensity has been recalculated. These notifications are created on your device. We don't send them from a server.

Where your data is stored

Match data is stored in the app on your iPhone and Apple Watch. The two devices sync directly with each other. Health data stays in Apple Health, under Apple's protections. GameSense doesn't upload either kind of data to our servers or to iCloud.

Data stored on your devices can be included in your own device backups, under your Apple account settings.

Service providers

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf and only to provide their service:

  • Apple handles App Store purchases, subscriptions, and Apple Health.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status. It receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device information, such as app version, OS version, and country or region. RevenueCat doesn't receive your health data or match data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we will never do

  • Sell your data.
  • Share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing, or use it to show you ads.
  • Use data from Apple Health for advertising, marketing, or data mining, or give it to data brokers.
  • Store your Apple Health data in iCloud.

We will disclose information only if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order. We will share no more than is required.

Planned features

In the future, we may add optional features that need data to leave your device. Examples:

  • User accounts, so you can back up and restore your matches or use them on another device.
  • Server-side processing, to calculate statistics and insights that are too heavy to run on the phone.
  • Logs and diagnostics, such as crash reports and error logs, to find and fix problems.
  • Product analysis, to understand which features are used and how to improve the app. Where possible, this will use aggregated or de-identified data.

If we introduce any of these, we promise to:

  • update this policy, and tell you in the app, before the change takes effect
  • ask for your explicit consent before sending any health data to our servers
  • use that data only to provide and improve GameSense, never for advertising, and never sell it
  • encrypt data in transit and at rest, and use only service providers that are contractually bound to protect it
  • let you delete your account and all the data linked to it

Your choices and rights

  • Health access: turn individual data types on or off in Health → Sharing → Apps → GameSense.
  • Delete your data: delete matches in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device. Workouts the app saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.
  • Notifications: manage them in iPhone Settings → Notifications → GameSense.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel them in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it is processed. Today almost all of your data is only on your device, so you already control it directly. For anything else, including purchase data held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help.

Children

GameSense is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If a change is significant, such as any change in how health data is handled, we will also tell you in the app and ask for your consent where required.
